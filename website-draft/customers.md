# A new food story, shaped by the people who eat it

Intan Isle is exploring rabbit as a possible additional meat choice for Singapore diners and food businesses. We want the first conversation to begin with flavour, familiarity and honest feedback.

## What we are exploring

Chef-led concepts include saddle cuts, sausage, nuggets, patties, broths and a rabbit shepherd's pie. These are product-development ideas, not products available to order. We are collecting feedback from households, chefs, caterers, hotels, retailers and food-service teams on taste expectations, preparation, portion, documentation, sourcing and price.

## Why speak with us?

The founders bring healthcare and culinary-industry experience (20 years each, as stated by the founders). Our approach is staged: listen first, confirm a lawful and welfare-sound pathway, then test recipes and customer response only when sourcing, venue, food-safety, consent and allergen controls are ready.

Explore [global rabbit recipes and food-history notes](rabbit-recipes.md). The collection spans traditions and modern dishes across multiple regions; it is a cultural sampler, not proof of current local demand or endorsement.

## Tell us what would make a trial worthwhile

Which format sounds most appealing? What would make rabbit a fit—or not a fit—for your household, menu or customers? What provenance, handling, price and delivery information would you need?
