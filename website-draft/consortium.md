# A practical consortium for a new food pathway

Intan Isle wants to explore a working group that brings together food businesses, researchers, educators, veterinarians, animal-welfare specialists, processors, suppliers, logistics teams and relevant public agencies.

## Shared work, clearly bounded

The proposed collaboration would identify what must be true before a pilot: a lawful operating route; suitable site and animal-care plan; traceable supply and processing; food-safety controls; buyer discovery; transparent economics; and an evidence plan for nutrition and environmental questions.

We hope to contribute documented questions, pilot evidence and draft operating procedures for competent authorities and institutions to review. Government agencies retain their statutory roles and decisions. No agency, university, polytechnic, doctor, dietitian, business or other named party is represented here as a partner or endorser without written evidence.

Potential first contribution: a 30-minute scoping conversation to identify one useful question, one responsible lead and one measurable next step. Space at NUS or Republic Polytechnic is requested for discussion only; access is unconfirmed.
