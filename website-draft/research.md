# Research questions for a responsible feasibility pilot

Intan Isle welcomes exploratory dialogue with research investigators in food science, nutrition, veterinary science, animal welfare, agriculture, engineering, business and consumer research.

## Potential questions to scope

- What product-specific composition and food-safety data are meaningful for a first lawful product concept?
- How should welfare, heat management, housing, biosecurity and veterinary oversight be assessed in Singapore conditions?
- What is the fair comparison for edible protein sold per unit of time and area, including feed, breeding stock, losses, processing yield, energy and labour?
- What buyer research would distinguish curiosity from willingness to purchase and repeat?
- Which approvals, ethics review, consent, biosafety or institutional permissions apply to proposed work?

No animal, human, tasting or clinical study is underway or approved by virtue of this page. Nutrition composition alone would not establish a health outcome or treatment effect. The team seeks advice on a feasible scope and appropriate review route. Space discussions at NUS or Republic Polytechnic are a request only; no access or affiliation is confirmed.
