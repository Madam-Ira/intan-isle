# Build the evidence before scaling the idea

Intan Isle is assessing a Singapore-origin rabbit-protein platform that could connect food, culinary product development, responsible farming and nutrition research. The opportunity is to test a new pathway—not to assume that a market, farm or regulatory route is already proven.

## The investment question

Can a lawful, welfare-sound and buyer-led pilot demonstrate a repeatable product, credible supply, transparent unit economics and a route to scale? We will investigate space efficiency and local production without claiming rabbit is part of Singapore's 2035 protein targets or that it outperforms other livestock.

## Stage gates

1. Buyer discovery and documented regulatory-pathway questions.
2. Site, veterinary, supply, processing and welfare feasibility.
3. Costed minimum pilot, with quotes and cash-flow scenarios.
4. Approved pilot and product-specific safety, composition and buyer testing.
5. Repeat-demand and unit-economics evidence before expansion.

No fixed raise, runway or return forecast is asserted on this page. Historic cost scenarios were different scopes and are not cumulative funding requirements. Market sizing will use verifiable buyer counts, order sizes, purchase frequency, net price and lawful supply capacity (TAM/SAM/SOM).

The founders are Nurtahirah and Fajrin Shahrom; each reports 20 years of experience in healthcare and culinary industries, respectively. Future expansion—including China, Brazil, Indonesia/Malaysia and GCC—is a research hypothesis requiring country-specific demand, regulatory, welfare, logistics and economics validation. TAMUK is a candidate breed strategy to test, not a proven advantage.
