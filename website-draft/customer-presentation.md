# Intan Isle | Customer presentation

## Could rabbit fit a Singapore meal?

This is a five-minute conversation about food ideas and buyer needs. Intan Isle is at discovery stage. **There is no product available to buy, no price or launch date, and no order is being taken.**

### 1. What we are exploring

Could rabbit become another thoughtfully prepared food choice in Singapore? We want to learn from the people who cook, serve and eat before developing any product.

### 2. Food ideas to imagine—not products for sale

Picture a slow-cooked, herb-rich rabbit stew; a familiar shepherd’s pie; or a chef-shaped cut, sausage, patty, nugget or broth. These are concepts for future recipe and buyer testing, not available Intan Isle products.

### 3. Who we want to hear from

Home cooks, chefs, restaurants, caterers, hotels and retailers have different needs. We want to understand the format, preparation, portion, food-safety and provenance information, supply, delivery and price that would make a trial useful—or rule it out.

### 4. Why talk with us?

Founders Nurtahirah and Fajrin Shahrom report 20 years of experience each across healthcare and culinary work. We are taking a staged approach: listen first, confirm a lawful and welfare-sound route, then consider controlled product and customer tests.

### 5. Help shape the next step

Which idea sounds most appealing? What would make you hesitate? What would you need to know before considering a future trial? A “no” is useful feedback too.
